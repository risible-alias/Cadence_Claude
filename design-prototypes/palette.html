Paper, ink, and five inks more

The proposed colour system for Cadence, in both appearances. Every value on this page is read live from tokens.css, and each contrast figure is measured against the paper beneath it.

  1. Colour means category. An activity keeps its ink in lists, on the timeline and in every chart.
  2. Status is never colour alone. Running, paused and completed differ in shape, line and wording.
  3. Totals are plain ink. Anything that sums across categories is drawn neutral.
  4. Words stay in ink. A coloured mark sits beside a name; the name itself is never coloured.
